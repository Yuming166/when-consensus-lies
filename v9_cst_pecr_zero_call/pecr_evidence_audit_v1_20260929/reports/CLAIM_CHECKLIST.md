# Claim-permission checklist

## Allowed with explicit qualifiers
- “On the repeatedly used 1,735-item development analysis, Curve HGB had higher AUROC than ordinary three-world HGB.”
- “The audited three-world Curve implementation passed the recorded zero-call array/invariance tests.”
- “The dataset flow retained 2,225 mechanical candidates and 2,223 items with JSON present in all three worlds, before strict analysis reduced the set to 1,735.”
- “The project lead reports 50/50 VALID in a blind review sample; the audit independently verifies only the existence and design of the blind packet.”

## Do not claim from this audit
- Do not call 1,735 items an independent test, confirmation set, or untouched holdout.
- Do not claim all 2,225 reverse constructions are semantically or financially valid.
- Do not claim Transformer superiority, mechanism-specific ablation support, or SOTA: the full Transformer is below Curve HGB and shuffled/no-mechanism ablations are close.
- Do not report the V1 Curve value 0.8092 as the audited current result; preserve it only as a historical pre-audit value.
- Do not claim the Curve confidence interval generalizes beyond the development queue or corrects repeated method selection.
- Do not state 100% coverage without the denominator “within the strict 1,735-item analysis set.”
